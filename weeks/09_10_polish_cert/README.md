# Weeks 9-10: Polish and optional certification (outline, not a guided lesson)

> Honest note: skeleton only. Cut scope here first if you fall behind. The optional exam prep goes first.

## Goal
Sound confident and honest in interviews, and have resume bullets that use the posting's keywords truthfully.

## Concepts
- Say what you built, what you learned and what you would do next. Never claim production experience

## Steps
1. Two mock technical interviews from `interview/pyspark_questions.md` and `interview/sql_questions.md` (20 minutes each, answer aloud).
2. One behavioral mock using `interview/behavioral_notes.md` (client success and construction superintendent stories).
3. Resume bullets: 4 bullets about the capstone using Databricks-style keywords you actually practiced (PySpark, Delta Lake, medallion, data quality, CI, Terraform validate).
4. Pick 3 questions from `interview/questions_to_ask.md` for Murphy USA.
5. Optional: skim the Databricks Data Engineer Associate exam guide and map each topic to a week you did.

## How to check your work
You can answer every question in the two question files in under two minutes, aloud, without notes.

## Self-test questions
1. Explain bronze/silver/gold to a non-technical manager in 30 seconds.
2. What is your honest answer to "have you used Databricks in production"?
3. Which project decision are you proudest of, and why?
